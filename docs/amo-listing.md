## Name

```
Quick Actions for Sankaku
```

## Summary (до 250 символов)

```
Rate and favorite posts straight from the grid, use keyboard shortcuts, upload
several files at once and hide ads on Sankaku Complex. Unofficial add-on, not
affiliated with the site.
```

## Description

```
Quick Actions for Sankaku adds the things the site makes you open a post for.

In the grid and in the viewer:
• click the rating stars or the favorite heart without opening the post;
• arrow keys move the selection, 1–5 rate it, F adds it to favorites;
• an optional badge shows the rating you gave;
• hidden previews can be revealed after a delay you choose.

On a post page: 1–5 to rate, F for favorites, C opens the comments with the
cursor already in the field, E picks an emotion.

Bulk upload: a separate page that fills the site's own upload form for several
files at once — tags for the whole selection, drag-and-drop ordering, parent
and book IDs, publishing one post after another.

Also optional: hiding ads and "Sankaku Plus" reminders, and counters for site
points and reputation next to the menu button.

The interface follows the language you picked in the site's settings —
26 languages are included.

Nothing is collected or sent anywhere: the add-on only stores your settings in
the browser. It works with your own account and the site's own API, exactly as
the site's own pages do.

Open source: https://github.com/MotoIlyuha/sankaku-quick-actions

This is an unofficial add-on. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or
endorsed by the operators of Sankaku Complex; the name is used only to say
which site the add-on works with.
```

## Поля, которые заполняются при подаче

1. **Notes for reviewers** — вставить содержимое `docs/REVIEWER_NOTES.md` и
   дописать логин и пароль тестового аккаунта.
2. **Source code** — загрузить `dist/sankaku-source-<версия>.zip`
   (`python build.py --source`).
3. **Data collection** — в манифесте уже объявлено «none», в форме выбрать то
   же самое.
